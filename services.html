Our Service Specialties

Every technician on our team is fully vetted, background-checked, and safety-certified. We deliver reliable commercial and residential solutions with clear upfront pricing and professional cleanup on every single visit.

Drywall & Painting

Flawless patch repairs, texture matching, and precise painting. We fix holes, water damage, stress cracks, and baseboard imperfections to restore structural integrity.

  • Water Damage Remediation
  • Seamless Texture Matching

Electrical Fixtures

Safe replacement of ceiling fans, modern light fixtures, smart doorbells, switches, and dimmers. Built on a foundation of strict electrical safety standards.

  • Light & Fan Installation
  • Smart Home Setup

Plumbing Specialties

Rapid diagnosis and fix of leaky faucets, running toilets, garbage disposals, and new sink hardware installations. Guarding your home against costly water intrusion.

  • Faucet & Toilet Upgrades
  • Garbage Disposal Repair

Doors & Hardware

Adjustment of misaligned entry doors, replacement of worn hinges, installation of heavy-duty security deadbolts, and smart electronic lock integration.

  • Lockset & Deadbolt Install
  • Hinge & Alignment Tuning

Home Safety Inspections

Proactive testing and replacement of smoke detectors, carbon monoxide monitors, dryer vent clear-outs, and installation of ADA-compliant grab bars.

  • ADA Grab Bar Mounts
  • Dryer Vent Unclogging

Custom Assembly

Professional assembly of flat-pack furniture, heavy shelving units, gym equipment, and secure wall-mounting for oversized flat-screen televisions.

  • Secure TV & Shelf Mounting
  • Large Flat-Pack Assembly
